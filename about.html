About

Why this site exists, how to get the most out of it, and answers to common questions.

Why this exists

Fitness Grind was built to make quality fitness and nutrition information simple, visual and free. There's no shortage of workout advice online, but most of it is either buried behind a sign-up form or too vague to actually use. This site skips both problems: open a page, find an exercise or a meal, and see exactly what you need — proper form, target muscles, calories and macros — without an account, a subscription or an app download.

It's meant as a reference, not a replacement for a coach or a dietitian. Use it to learn the basics, then build your own routine around what fits your goals and schedule.

How to use it

Three simple steps to get value out of the site.

1

Browse exercises

Filter by muscle group on the Exercises page and open any card for step-by-step form instructions.

2

Check the meals

Look through the Meals page to find balanced options and see their full macro breakdown.

3

Build your own plan

Combine what you've learned into a routine and diet that actually fits your life.

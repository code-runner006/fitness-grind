Chest
Beginner

Push-Ups

A bodyweight staple that builds chest, shoulder and tricep strength.

Target muscles

Chest, shoulders, triceps

Equipment

None — bodyweight only

How to perform

  1. Start in a plank position, hands slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Lower your chest toward the floor by bending your elbows.
  4. Push back up to the starting position.

Tip

Keep your core tight so your hips don't sag.

Chest
Intermediate

Bench Press

A classic barbell lift for building raw chest and pressing strength.

Target muscles

Chest, shoulders, triceps

Equipment

Barbell, bench

How to perform

  1. Lie on the bench with feet flat on the floor.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width.
  3. Lower the bar to your mid-chest with control.
  4. Press the bar back up until your arms are extended.

Tip

Keep your shoulder blades pinched together throughout the lift.

Back
Intermediate

Pull-Ups

A demanding bodyweight move that builds a wide, strong back.

Target muscles

Back, biceps

Equipment

Pull-up bar

How to perform

  1. Hang from the bar, palms facing away, hands shoulder-width apart.
  2. Pull your body up until your chin clears the bar.
  3. Lower yourself back down with control.
  4. Repeat without swinging.

Tip

Focus on pulling with your back, not just your arms.

Back
Intermediate

Bent-Over Rows

Builds back thickness and improves posture under load.

Target muscles

Back, biceps

Equipment

Barbell or dumbbells

How to perform

  1. Hinge at the hips with knees slightly bent, back flat.
  2. Hold the weight with arms extended toward the floor.
  3. Pull the weight toward your lower ribs.
  4. Lower with control and repeat.

Tip

Avoid rounding your lower back during the movement.

Legs
Beginner

Squats

The foundation of leg training, working nearly every lower-body muscle.

Target muscles

Quads, glutes, hamstrings

Equipment

Bodyweight, or barbell for added load

How to perform

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Bend your knees and push your hips back as if sitting in a chair.
  3. Lower until your thighs are parallel to the floor.
  4. Push through your heels to stand back up.

Tip

Keep your chest up and knees tracking over your toes.

Legs
Beginner

Lunges

Builds single-leg strength and balance.

Target muscles

Quads, glutes, hamstrings

Equipment

Bodyweight, or dumbbells for added load

How to perform

  1. Step forward with one leg.
  2. Lower your hips until both knees are bent around 90 degrees.
  3. Push back up through the front heel to the starting position.
  4. Repeat on the other leg.

Tip

Keep your front knee aligned with your ankle, not past your toes.

Arms
Beginner

Bicep Curls

An isolation move that targets the front of the upper arm.

Target muscles

Biceps

Equipment

Dumbbells

How to perform

  1. Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand, arms extended.
  2. Curl the weights toward your shoulders, keeping elbows tucked in.
  3. Squeeze at the top.
  4. Lower slowly back to the start.

Tip

Avoid swinging your body to lift the weight.

Arms
Intermediate

Tricep Dips

Targets the back of the upper arm using just a bench or chair.

Target muscles

Triceps, shoulders

Equipment

Bench or sturdy chair

How to perform

  1. Sit on the edge of a bench, hands gripping the edge beside your hips.
  2. Slide your hips off the bench, legs extended.
  3. Bend your elbows to lower your body toward the floor.
  4. Push back up until your arms are straight.

Tip

Keep your elbows pointing backward, not flared out.

Core
Beginner

Plank

A simple isometric hold that builds core stability.

Target muscles

Core, shoulders

Equipment

None

How to perform

  1. Get into a forearm plank position, elbows under your shoulders.
  2. Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Engage your core and hold the position.
  4. Breathe steadily throughout.

Tip

Don't let your hips sag or pike upward.

Core
Beginner

Russian Twists

A rotational move that targets the obliques.

Target muscles

Obliques, core

Equipment

Bodyweight, or a light weight plate

How to perform

  1. Sit on the floor with knees bent, leaning back slightly.
  2. Lift your feet off the ground for added difficulty.
  3. Twist your torso to touch the floor on each side.
  4. Keep your core engaged throughout.

Tip

Move with control rather than speed for better activation.

Cardio
Beginner

Jump Rope

A simple, effective way to raise your heart rate and build coordination.

Target muscles

Calves, shoulders, core

Equipment

Jump rope

How to perform

  1. Hold the rope handles at hip height.
  2. Swing the rope overhead and jump as it passes under your feet.
  3. Land softly on the balls of your feet.
  4. Keep a steady rhythm.

Tip

Use small, quick wrist rotations rather than big arm swings.

Cardio
Advanced

Burpees

A demanding full-body move that combines strength and cardio.

Target muscles

Full body

Equipment

None

How to perform

  1. Start standing, then drop into a squat with hands on the floor.
  2. Kick your feet back into a plank position.
  3. Perform a push-up, then jump your feet back to your hands.
  4. Explosively jump up with arms overhead.

Tip

Move at a controlled pace at first to keep good form under fatigue.
